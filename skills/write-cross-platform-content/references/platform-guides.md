# Platform Guides

Use only the requested or selected platform sections. Check current official rules when account eligibility, disclosure, AI policy, field limits, or submission mechanics matter.

## Medium

Role: durable explanation, founder insight, tutorial, comparison, or evidence-backed synthesis.

Produce:

- three to five title options and one subtitle;
- a complete article with a strong first paragraph, descriptive headings, limitations, and one CTA;
- up to five relevant topic tags;
- a short excerpt/meta description;
- a cover-image brief and optional inline figure ideas;
- a canonical/import note when the source first appeared on an owned site;
- an AI-assistance disclosure note only when current policy or editorial context requires it.

Write an article, not a product landing page. Use a narrative only when there is a real event or decision behind it. In a comparison, state the comparison date, consistent dimensions, and which product suits which situation. Avoid unsupported “best,” speed, privacy, or security claims.

## Quora

Role: answer one concrete question completely and capture long-tail intent.

Produce:

- the exact question being answered;
- a direct first-paragraph answer;
- a structured explanation, steps, or decision criteria;
- alternatives and limitations;
- a natural disclosure when mentioning the team's product;
- one optional supporting link after the answer provides standalone value.

Do not write a generic product essay, self-create demand, repeat near-identical answers, or leave a link-only response. Preserve truthful credentials and affiliations.

## X

Role: test a sharp insight, ask a concrete product question, show a result, or start a focused conversation.

Produce one or both as appropriate:

- a standalone short post within the current character limit;
- a four-to-seven-post thread where every post advances the idea;
- a first reply for the product link when discussion is the primary goal;
- one follow-up question that distinguishes concrete behaviors or tradeoffs.

Prefer a specific choice over “What do you think?” For product research, ask users to choose between observable behaviors and explain why. Use no more than a few necessary hashtags; default to none. Do not repeat the same promotion, mass reply, or misrepresent a single response as market validation.

## Reddit

Role: community-specific discussion, experience report, useful answer, or product discovery research.

Before drafting, identify a plausible subreddit and inspect its current rules, pinned posts, self-promotion policy, and title conventions. Produce:

- subreddit fit and risk note;
- two title options in the community's style;
- a self-contained body led by the problem, experience, or question;
- maker/team disclosure when applicable;
- link placement that follows the community's rules;
- two or three likely comment replies.

Do not cross-post identical copy, disguise promotion as an independent recommendation, ask for upvotes, or repost after removal without understanding the reason. If no suitable community is known, draft a community-neutral version and mark subreddit selection as blocking publication.

## Hacker News

Role: technically substantive feedback on a working artifact, engineering story, or genuine industry discussion.

Apply a readiness gate before drafting a submission:

- the account and submission type are eligible;
- the artifact is usable or understandable now;
- the author can answer technical questions;
- there are meaningful design choices, implementation details, or lessons;
- the submission is not merely a marketing campaign.

When ready, produce:

- a factual title using `Show HN:` only when eligible and appropriate;
- a concise submission text explaining what was built, why, how it works, tradeoffs, and known limits;
- a clear maker disclosure;
- anticipated technical questions and factual answers.

Do not ask for votes, use marketing adjectives, hide limitations, or bypass account-age/moderation restrictions by changing the title or submission category.

## Product Hunt

Role: launch page, product update, maker story, FAQ, and direct response to launch-day feedback.

Produce only the artifacts relevant to the request:

- name, tagline, short description, and launch topics within current field limits;
- maker comment covering problem, solution, current state, limitations, and requested feedback;
- FAQ and concise comment replies;
- gallery/video messaging and launch CTA;
- a Product Hunt update when this is a real new milestone rather than recycled promotion.

Reply to the user's actual point before explaining the current product. Treat a comment as one qualitative signal, not proof of widespread demand. Never buy, exchange, reward, or directly require upvotes.

## GitHub

Role: technical proof, documentation, support, release communication, and open collaboration.

First choose the native artifact:

- README or docs for setup and concepts;
- Release notes for shipped changes;
- Discussion for an open technical/design question;
- Issue response for a specific problem;
- repository announcement only when the repository itself is relevant.

Produce exact Markdown for that artifact, including prerequisites, steps, compatibility, data behavior, limitations, and feedback/contribution path. Use few adjectives and concrete technical language. Never advertise in unrelated repositories or buy/exchange stars.

## Dev.to

Role: developer tutorial, build log, technical lesson, open-source update, or practical engineering discussion.

Produce:

- three title options aimed at a concrete developer problem or lesson;
- a complete article with prerequisites, implementation or reasoning, code/examples when verified, tradeoffs, and a useful conclusion;
- relevant tags within the platform's current allowance;
- a cover-image brief and concise series placement when this belongs to an ongoing sequence;
- frontmatter/canonical guidance when the article was first published elsewhere;
- a clear maker, employer, product, sponsorship, or AI disclosure when applicable.

The article must remain independently useful if every product link is removed. Prefer reproducible details, real constraints, and lessons learned over feature lists. Avoid low-value SEO reposts, generic AI-written tutorials, irrelevant tags, excessive product promotion, and publishing a duplicate without the correct canonical/original-source handling. Verify current editor, frontmatter, tag, organization, and disclosure rules before publication when they affect the draft.

## Hashnode

Role: owned developer publication, technical tutorial, engineering story, build log, or multi-part series with community distribution.

Produce:

- three title options, one subtitle, and a clean slug;
- a complete technical article with prerequisites, steps or architecture, examples, limitations, and conclusion;
- relevant tags, a cover-image brief, and an optional series/newsletter placement;
- canonical/original URL guidance and a recommendation for which domain should be the authoritative source;
- a brief product/team disclosure and one developer-relevant CTA.

Use Hashnode as a durable developer publication, especially when an owned or custom-domain blog is the canonical source. Do not create duplicate indexing between the website, Hashnode, Medium, and Dev.to. Avoid generic AI articles, shallow rewrites, and product-only announcements with no technical value. Verify current publication, custom-domain, canonical, import, newsletter, and AI/disclosure behavior before publishing when those controls matter.

## HackerNoon

Role: editorial technology publication for substantive engineering analysis, founder lessons, industry insight, or evidence-backed technology stories.

HackerNoon is not Hacker News. HackerNoon is an editorial publication with contributor and review requirements; Hacker News is a community discussion and link-submission forum. Do not reuse a `Show HN` title or short Hacker News submission as a HackerNoon story.

Produce:

- three editorial headline options and one dek;
- a pitch/angle stating why the story matters now and what the reader will learn;
- a complete story with evidence, sources, concrete examples, tradeoffs, and a non-promotional conclusion;
- relevant tags, an image brief, and source/citation notes;
- an author bio plus maker, employer, sponsorship, product, and AI-assistance disclosures when applicable;
- editorial submission notes covering originality, prior publication, content rights, and any required revisions.

Expect editorial review and possible revision requests. Prefer a defensible thesis and real experience over a launch announcement. Avoid press releases, sales copy, unsupported claims, disguised sponsorship, unclear image/text rights, and unmarked republishing. Verify current contributor eligibility, AI-content policy, disclosure, originality/republishing, rights, and submission rules before submission.

## SEO / Owned Blog

Role: satisfy a stable search intent and become the canonical, maintainable source.

Produce:

- primary intent and keyword, plus a small set of secondary terms;
- search-aligned title options, slug, meta title, and meta description;
- article outline followed by the complete article when requested;
- direct answer near the top, scannable steps or comparison dimensions, FAQ, and one CTA;
- internal-link suggestions and external sources;
- schema candidates only when the page genuinely qualifies;
- last-reviewed date and update triggers for volatile comparisons.

Avoid keyword stuffing, doorway pages, unverified comparison tables, and scaled low-value pages. The product may appear as one fitting solution, but the page must solve the searcher's task without requiring a click to another page.

## YouTube

Role: demonstrate the workflow, establish trust through real operation, or explain a decision visually.

Produce:

- three to five truthful title options;
- thumbnail concept with short on-image copy;
- a 0-15 second hook showing the problem and promised result;
- a scene-by-scene script with narration, visuals, screen actions, and evidence/limitation callouts;
- description, chapters, one CTA, and disclosure notes;
- one to three Shorts concepts derived from self-contained moments.

Prefer real product footage, tests, diagrams, and readable UI. Do not use misleading thumbnails, hide paid conditions, claim untested results, or fill the video with generic promotional footage. Confirm music, footage, logo, and likeness rights.

## Fallback platform

For a requested platform not listed here, research its current official rules and dominant native formats. Define its user intent, suitable artifact, opening convention, link/CTA behavior, disclosure requirement, and primary redlines before drafting. Do not treat another platform's copy as the final version.
