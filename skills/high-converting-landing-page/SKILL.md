# High-Converting Landing Page Framework

A battle-tested methodology for building clean, trustworthy, high-converting product landing pages. It replaces decorative corporate fluff and generic SaaS AI templates with evidence-based, proof-first narrative architecture.

## 1. The 5-Second Clarity Rule

Within 5 seconds of landing on the page, the user must understand:
1. **What concrete result does this give me?** (Not technical protocols or abstract claims).
2. **Who is it for and what does it look like?** (Real UI screenshot or video, not 3D stock abstract orbs).
3. **What is my low-friction first step?** (Clean CTA: Download, Free Trial, Sign Up).

## 2. The 7-Step Conversion Narrative Sequence

Structure the page to guide the user's psychological decision flow rather than dumping features:

```text
1. Result Clarity (Hero)           → What outcome do I get?
2. Proof & Signals (Proof Strip)   → Can I see evidence it actually works?
3. Daily Scenarios (Highlights)    → Does it fit my specific everyday workflow?
4. Mechanism (How It Works)        → Is the solution intuitive and understandable?
5. Trust & Control (Security)      → Who has access? Can I trust it with my data?
6. Pricing & Low-Friction Start    → What does it cost, and what are the trial terms?
7. Objection Handling (FAQ)        → Are my lingering doubts resolved?
```

## 3. Recommended 15-Section Homepage Hierarchy

1. **Header:** Sticky, clean brand logo, 5-6 core anchor links, language switch, primary CTA button.
2. **Hero:** Eyebrow + outcome-driven H1 + 1-2 sentence subheadline + CTA buttons + platform support line + real desktop/mobile UI frame.
3. **Proof Strip:** 6-8 compact signal pills (Key capability, speed, privacy, trial policy).
4. **Highlights / Use Cases:** 3-4 lifestyle/workflow cards mapping directly to target personas.
5. **Feature 1 (Core Value):** Deep dive into the primary feature with interactive or visual proof.
6. **Feature 2 (Mobility / Secondary Value):** Out-of-office, mobile access, or collaboration workflow.
7. **Feature 3 (Control & Governance):** Device management, connection pairing, audit logs.
8. **Comparison Card:** "Old/Legacy Detour" vs "Your Product Direct Path" side-by-side diagram.
9. **Use Cases & Workflows:** Real-world applications to inspire non-obvious usage.
10. **Product Gallery:** High-resolution screenshots of key product views with concise captions.
11. **Platform Support:** Explicit desktop vs mobile roles and OS compatibility matrix.
12. **Pricing Plan:** Clear tier with free trial, included features, and transparent billing notes.
13. **FAQ Accordion:** Answer the 6-8 top pre-download objections honestly.
14. **Final CTA:** High-contrast full-width section with primary action buttons.
15. **Footer:** Copyright, legal disclaimers, privacy policy, and key navigation links.

## 4. Copywriting & Fact Boundary Rules

- **Lead with Outcomes:**
  - ❌ *Bad:* "Utilizes high-concurrency P2P WebRTC tunneling protocols."
  - ✅ *Good:* "Phone photos land in your computer folder automatically."
- **Honest Constraints:**
  - State boundaries plainly (e.g., "Remote access requires the host computer to stay online").
  - Do NOT use exaggerated claims like "Zero-Knowledge", "Unbreakable", "Lifetime Free", or "Completely replaces cloud storage" unless strictly proven and documented in `templates/product-fact-sheet.md`.

## 5. Responsive Acceptance Checklist

Before publishing, verify across all three viewports:
- [ ] **Mobile 320px:** No horizontal overflow, buttons stack cleanly, text does not clip.
- [ ] **Mobile 390px (iPhone standard):** Hero title fits within 2-3 lines, CTAs are easily tappable.
- [ ] **Desktop 1440px:** Hero media does not overpower or crowd headline; layout max-width is constrained (~1240px container).
- [ ] **Motion & Performance:** Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; LCP under 2.5s; all screenshots legible.
